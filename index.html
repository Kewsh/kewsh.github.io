About

I'm currently a graduate researcher in Software Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, where I'm advised by Prof. Mohammad Izadi.

I'm interested in all things computer systems, with a current focus on microsecond-scale CPU scheduling. I also have a couple of years of industry experience building software across the entire stack.

Outside of work, I watch a lot of football and play some CS2.
